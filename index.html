        The transformer

        Right now q speaks from the field alone: bigrams, trigrams and Hebbian links counted in its corpus, bent by the chambers. Its transformer is silent, and destiny and the parliament have nothing to steer with, because both read token embeddings and there are none without weights. The engine says this itself in its log.

        The corpus on this page is the first 16 KB of q.txt, the text the weights were trained on: 84 paragraphs. The native engines read all 439 KB; the browser engine would hold this page for over half a minute doing the same.

        The weights are 1,653,894 parameters (6.6 MB): three layers, six heads of RRPRAM and Janus echo attention, trained 200K steps on q.txt. Load them and ε and αδ wake up.

        How q works

        ε, the substrate

        Each layer runs three attention mechanisms mixed by a learned gate: content attention (QKᵀ), RRPRAM, which scores positions straight from x·W_r, and Janus echo, where the weight matrix attends to itself. A second gate keeps the transformer silent until its logits are strong: clamp((mean|logit| − 0.5) / 1.5, 0, 1). Vocabulary 1280, width 192, 3 layers, context 128. At equal size RRPRAM reached loss 2.41 where content attention reached 2.86.

        γ, the field

        When q wakes it counts bigrams, trigrams, Hebbian co-occurrences and unigrams in its corpus, and it keeps counting every token it speaks and every word you give it. Each token's score adds these tables with coefficients the chambers bend: with weights 0.6, 0.4, 0.3, 5 and 3 for Hebbian, prophecy, destiny, bigram and trigram; without weights 1.0, 0.7, 0.15, 15 and 10.

        αδ, the parliament

        Four rank-4 LoRA experts vote on every token. When they disagree the election admits more voices, when they agree fewer. They learn from prophecy debt by a Hebbian rule with no backward pass, consolidate what accumulates, split when one dominates too long and die after sustained silence. α = 0.05 is the weight of their voice.

        Chambers

        Fear, love, rage, void, flow and complexity decay at their own rates and pull on each other through Kuramoto coupling. Words feed them: anchor words, a body lexicon (chest, throat, warmth, burning, tremor) and harmful words, which leave a scar. A scar raises trauma, leans the chambers toward fear and void and makes wormholes rarer.

        θ, the chain

        Twelve steps. Backward steps start from random corpus sentences, the pivot turns, forward steps start where destiny points: of 50 candidate openings, the one closest to the destiny vector wins. The split moves with debt and with the drift between the Hebrew and Gregorian calendars, and temperature breathes at the Schumann frequency, 7.83 Hz. Afterwards sentence phonon attention finds the weakest sentence and reseeds it from its neighbour.

        Memory

        When a chain ends, q writes its field, chambers, periodic table and phase memory to this browser's storage, and it reads them back when it wakes, so a second visit starts from a changed organism. Memory written under another vocabulary keeps its words and chambers and leaves its token tables out. The native engines keep the same organ in q.sqlite.
